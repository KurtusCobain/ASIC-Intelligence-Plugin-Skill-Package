Diagnostic trust contract

Make uncertainty visible.

PROVENANCE · FRESHNESS · CONFLICTS

Evidence integrity

Important claims should trace to supplied evidence, preserve age/freshness, and keep disagreements visible.

UNKNOWNS · CONFIDENCE

No invented certainty

Missing remains Unknown. Confidence is explicit, and “Insufficient Evidence” is allowed when the evidence cannot support a conclusion.

COMMON CAUSE · NEXT CHECK

Operational reasoning

Shared infrastructure and competing explanations are considered before isolated failures, followed by the safest high-value next observation.

Hard boundary

AI that investigates. Not AI with a kill switch to your mine.

Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter reasons over evidence supplied to the AI session. It does not add a miner-control backend or privileged remote-management path.

  • No reboot or reset execution
  • No pool/wallet changes
  • No firmware flashing
  • No voltage/frequency tuning
  • No PDU or breaker switching
  • No network reconfiguration
  • No arbitrary state-changing shell execution

FAQ

What this product is—and what it isn't.

Does it connect directly to a mining site?

No. v1.1.0 reasons over evidence supplied to the AI session and ships no hosted collection or mining-control backend.

Is the public demo evidence real?

No. The five release scenarios are synthetic. Private real-world validation material is not published here.

Is ASIC Intelligence Desktop the same product?

No. Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter is standalone. ASIC Intelligence Desktop is a separate product in development.

Why does the repository still use the name ASIC Intelligence Plugin/Skill Package?

That is the existing public repository/package name. The user-facing product is Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter, powered by ASIC Intelligence.

Can I reuse or resell the package?

The public package is source-available under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0. Use, modification, and redistribution are subject to that license. See the license terms or contact austin@wnclogiclab.com.

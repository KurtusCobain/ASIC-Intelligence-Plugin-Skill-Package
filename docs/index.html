Bring what you already have

Evidence in. Diagnosis out.

You do not need to connect a mining account or hand the tool control of your miners. Supply the evidence you want analyzed.

MINER LOGS

Kernel and runtime output

Startup, thermal, fan, board/chip, PSU, pool, and other miner-reported evidence.

FLEET EXPORTS

CSV, JSON, and XLSX

Compare cohorts, outliers, failures, Unknowns, and shared operating patterns across supplied records.

SCREENSHOTS

Status-page evidence

Analyze supplied miner status screens without pretending a screenshot is live telemetry.

TELEMETRY

Hashrate, temperature, fans, shares

Keep original units and distinguish explicit zero values from missing data.

NETWORK / POWER

Shared infrastructure evidence

Consider DNS, TCP, routes, rack, switch, PDU, breaker, and thermal evidence before mass hardware-failure claims.

REPAIR HISTORY

Symptoms, repairs, recurrence

Use supplied maintenance history to identify repeated patterns without inventing missing events.

Diagnostic trust contract

Evidence first. Inference second.

The Troubleshooter is designed to make the difference between an observation, a calculation, a hypothesis, an assumption, and a missing fact obvious.

PROVENANCE

Show where the claim came from

Important findings should trace back to supplied evidence rather than appearing as unsupported certainty.

FRESHNESS

Old evidence is not current telemetry

Historical or unknown-age evidence lowers confidence instead of silently acting like a live reading.

CONFLICTS

Disagreement stays visible

If two supplied sources disagree, the conflict stays visible rather than being silently resolved.

UNKNOWNS

Missing is not zero

Blank, unavailable, unparseable, and unknown values remain Unknown.

COMMON CAUSE

Check shared failures first

Shared rack, network, power, cooling, firmware, and timing patterns are considered before many isolated failures are claimed.

NEXT SAFE CHECK

Reduce uncertainty safely

The next step should be the read-only or observational check that best separates competing explanations.

Hard boundary

AI that investigates. Not AI with a kill switch to your mine.

Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter reasons over evidence supplied to the AI session. It does not add a miner-control backend or privileged remote-management path.

  • No reboot or reset execution
  • No pool/wallet changes
  • No firmware flashing
  • No voltage/frequency tuning
  • No PDU or breaker switching
  • No network reconfiguration
  • No arbitrary state-changing shell execution

Why it exists

Built from Bitcoin mining operations experience.

Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter grew from firsthand experience working around Bitcoin mining operations and building software for the diagnostic problems technicians and operations teams encounter.

Austin Bercaw-Bond progressed from technician to operations intelligence analyst across four promotions in three years and later developed proprietary software used in daily mining operations. FAQ

What this product is—and what it isn't.

Does it connect directly to a mining site?

No. v1.1.0 reasons over evidence supplied to the AI session and ships no hosted collection or mining-control backend.

Is the public demo evidence real?

No. The five release scenarios are synthetic. Private real-world validation material is not published here.

Is ASIC Intelligence Desktop the same product?

No. Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter is standalone. ASIC Intelligence Desktop is a separate product in development.

Does sponsorship influence diagnoses?

No. Public sponsorship funds public work. Commercial ASIC Intelligence development is a separate relationship. Diagnostic conclusions are expected to follow evidence either way.

Why does the repository still use the name ASIC Intelligence Plugin/Skill Package?

That is the existing public repository/package name. The user-facing product is Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter, powered by ASIC Intelligence.

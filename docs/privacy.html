Privacy Policy

This policy explains the data boundaries of the Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter v1.1.0 public plugin/skill package and this GitHub Pages website.

Back to main siteTerms of Service
Plugin data boundary

No ASIC Intelligence-hosted collection backend.

The v1.1.0 public package has no ASIC Intelligence backend, user-account system, authentication flow, hosted telemetry collector, or direct connection to a mining site. The package is designed to reason over evidence that you intentionally provide to the host AI environment, such as logs, fleet exports, screenshots, spreadsheets, repair records, or incident notes.

What the package itself does not do

  • Does not create an ASIC Intelligence account
  • Does not send mining evidence to an ASIC Intelligence server
  • Does not request miner, pool, wallet, or network credentials
  • Does not run background monitoring or site-wide data collection
  • Does not perform miner-control or state-changing actions
Host AI provider

Evidence is handled by the environment you choose to use.

When you use this plugin or skill in ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, or another compatible host, the evidence you intentionally provide is processed through that host environment. The host provider's privacy policy, account settings, retention rules, and enterprise controls may apply. This public package does not override those policies and does not independently receive a copy of your conversation or uploaded evidence.

Sanitize operational evidence

Before uploading files, remove passwords, API keys, MFA codes, private keys, seed phrases, wallet secrets, employee information, public IP addresses, or facility details that are not needed for diagnosis.

Use only authorized data

Only provide mining, network, repair, or infrastructure evidence that you are authorized to access and analyze.

Public issues are public

If you use the GitHub issue tracker for support, do not post unsanitized customer, credential, wallet, site, or employee information.

Website analytics

This public website uses Google Analytics.

The GitHub Pages site loads Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate site usage, such as page visits and general browser/device interaction. Google may receive analytics information when its tag loads, subject to Google's own privacy practices and your browser, network, or privacy controls.

The analytics used by this website are separate from the Bitcoin Mining Troubleshooter's diagnostic processing. The plugin itself does not add an ASIC Intelligence analytics or telemetry backend to your AI session.

Third-party hosting and links

This site is hosted with GitHub Pages, and downloads/support may link to GitHub. Those services may process ordinary web-request, account, or usage information under their own policies. External AI hosts likewise operate under their own privacy terms.

Retention and control

No separate mining-evidence retention by this public package.

Because v1.1.0 has no ASIC Intelligence-hosted evidence backend, this public package does not maintain a separate server-side copy of the mining evidence you provide to your AI host. Retention by the host AI provider, GitHub, or Google Analytics is governed by those services and their applicable settings.

Questions or requests

This policy may be updated when the plugin's architecture or public website changes. Material changes should be reflected on this page.

# Code of Conduct

Be professional, technical, and respectful.

Project discussions should focus on reproducible evidence, diagnostic reasoning, safe operating practice, and useful feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, credential exposure, or publication of another operator's private production data is not acceptable.

Disagreement about diagnoses is welcome when it is grounded in evidence. Challenge the conclusion, show the conflicting observation, and explain what additional measurement would resolve the disagreement.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that creates safety, privacy, or abuse risks for contributors or mining operators.
